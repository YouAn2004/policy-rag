# POL-14 Whistleblowing and Speak Up Policy

## 1. Reporting channel
1.1 Concerns about fraud, bribery, harassment, safety or other serious misconduct can be reported to ethics@meridianworks.example or the independent hotline.
1.2 Reports may be made anonymously.

## 2. Protection
2.1 No one who reports a concern in good faith will suffer retaliation.
2.2 The identity of the reporter is kept confidential as far as the law allows.
2.3 Knowingly making a false report is a disciplinary offence under POL-11.

## 3. Handling
3.1 The Ethics Committee acknowledges every report within 5 working days.
3.2 Investigations aim to conclude within 30 calendar days. The reporter is told of the outcome where possible.
