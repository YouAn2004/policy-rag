# POL-09 Performance Review and Promotion Policy (managers and HR)

## 1. Review cycle
1.1 Formal reviews are held twice a year, in June and December.
1.2 Managers complete the written assessment within 10 working days of the cycle opening.
1.3 Ratings use a 1 to 5 scale where 5 is outstanding and 3 meets expectations.

## 2. Calibration
2.1 Department heads hold a calibration session to keep ratings consistent across teams.
2.2 No more than 10 percent of a team may be rated 5.

## 3. Promotion
3.1 To be considered for promotion an employee must have at least 12 months in the current grade.
3.2 The employee must have received a rating of 4 or higher in two consecutive review cycles.
3.3 Promotions are approved by the department head and HR, and take effect on 1 April or 1 October.

## 4. Performance improvement plan
4.1 An employee rated 1 or 2 may be placed on a performance improvement plan (PIP) of 60 days.
4.2 The manager and employee meet at least every 2 weeks during a PIP.
4.3 An employee on a PIP is not eligible for promotion or the annual bonus under POL-10.
4.4 Failure to meet PIP targets may lead to action under POL-11.
