# POL-12 Procurement and Vendor Management Policy

## 1. Purchasing thresholds
1.1 Purchases below RM 5,000 need one quotation and manager approval.
1.2 Purchases from RM 5,000 to RM 50,000 need three written quotations and department head approval.
1.3 Purchases above RM 50,000 require a formal tender and approval from both the CFO and the CEO.
1.4 Splitting a purchase to avoid a threshold is prohibited.

## 2. Vendor onboarding
2.1 New vendors must be registered by the procurement team before the first purchase order.
2.2 Vendors that process company or personal data must pass an IT security assessment and sign a data processing agreement before onboarding.
2.3 Employees must declare any relationship with a vendor under POL-01 before taking part in selection.

## 3. Payment
3.1 Vendor invoices are paid within 45 days of receipt of a correct invoice.
3.2 Advance payments above RM 20,000 need CFO approval.
