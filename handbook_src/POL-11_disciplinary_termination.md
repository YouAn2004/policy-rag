# POL-11 Disciplinary, Grievance and Termination Policy (managers and HR)

## 1. Disciplinary process
1.1 The usual steps are a verbal warning, a written warning, a final written warning and then termination.
1.2 Serious misconduct such as fraud, violence or bribery may lead to immediate suspension and dismissal without prior warnings.
1.3 Managers must involve HR before issuing any written warning.

## 2. Investigation
2.1 HR starts an investigation within 14 calendar days of a complaint.
2.2 The employee has the right to hear the allegations, respond in writing and be accompanied by a colleague at any disciplinary meeting.
2.3 Suspension during an investigation is on full pay and limited to 30 days.

## 3. Probation and notice
3.1 Probation lasts 3 months and may be extended once by up to 3 months.
3.2 Notice during probation is 1 month.
3.3 Notice after confirmation is 2 months for Grades 1 to 5 and 3 months for Grades 6 and above.
3.4 The company may pay in lieu of notice.

## 4. Grievances
4.1 Employees may raise a grievance with their manager or, if the manager is involved, with HR.
4.2 HR acknowledges a grievance within 5 working days and aims to resolve it within 30 days.
