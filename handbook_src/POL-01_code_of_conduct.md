# POL-01 Code of Conduct (Meridian Works Sdn Bhd)

Meridian Works Sdn Bhd is a fictional company created for a software portfolio project. Nothing here is real policy or legal advice.

## 1. Purpose and scope
1.1 This Code applies to all employees, interns and contractors of Meridian Works.
1.2 Every person must act honestly, respect colleagues and protect company assets.

## 2. Respectful workplace
2.1 Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated in any form, in person or online.
2.2 Employees who experience or witness misconduct should report it to HR at hr@meridianworks.example or through the channel in POL-14.
2.3 Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a disciplinary offence.

## 3. Outside work and conflicts of interest
3.1 Employees must obtain written approval from their manager and HR before taking any paid outside employment.
3.2 Employees must declare any personal interest in a supplier, customer or competitor within 14 days of becoming aware of it.
3.3 An annual conflict of interest declaration is due every January.

## 4. Social media and confidentiality
4.1 Employees must not share confidential company or customer information on social media.
4.2 Personal views posted online must not be presented as the company's views.

## 5. Dress code
5.1 The standard dress code is smart casual. Client meetings may require business attire as advised by the meeting owner.

## 6. Policy precedence
6.1 If two policies conflict on the same point, the policy with the later effective date prevails.
6.2 If the effective dates are equal, the more specific policy prevails over the more general one.
6.3 A superseded policy has no force for events that happen after its replacement takes effect.
6.4 Questions about which policy applies should go to HR at hr@meridianworks.example.
