# POL-08 Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy

## 1. Zero tolerance
1.1 Employees must never offer, give, request or accept a bribe in any form.
1.2 Facilitation payments are prohibited.

## 2. Gifts and hospitality received
2.1 Gifts worth up to RM 200 may be accepted if they are occasional and not linked to a business decision.
2.2 Gifts worth more than RM 200 must be declared to Compliance in the gift register within 7 days.
2.3 Gifts worth more than RM 500 must be refused or returned. If return is not possible, the gift is handed to Compliance.
2.4 Cash and cash equivalents such as vouchers must never be accepted.

## 3. Gifts and hospitality given
3.1 Gifts to customers or suppliers are capped at RM 300 per recipient per year and need manager approval.
3.2 Gifts and hospitality for public officials are prohibited unless approved in writing by the Head of Compliance.

## 4. Reporting
4.1 Suspected bribery must be reported to compliance@meridianworks.example or through POL-14.
