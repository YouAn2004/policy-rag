# POL-04 Expense Claims Policy

## 1. General rules
1.1 Employees may claim reasonable business expenses incurred on behalf of the company.
1.2 Claims must be submitted in the expense system within 30 calendar days of the expense date. Late claims need CFO approval.
1.3 Original receipts or tax invoices are required for every expense above RM 25.
1.4 Approved claims are paid within 14 working days of approval.

## 2. Approval limits
2.1 Claims up to RM 2,000 are approved by the employee's direct manager.
2.2 Claims above RM 2,000 and up to RM 10,000 are approved by the department head.
2.3 Claims above RM 10,000 are approved by the CFO.
2.4 No one may approve their own claim.

## 3. Entertainment and gifts
3.1 Client entertainment is capped at RM 150 per person per occasion.
3.2 Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances.
3.3 Gifts to third parties follow POL-08.

## 4. Meals and transport
4.1 Meals while travelling domestically on business are reimbursed up to RM 60 per day.
4.2 Mileage for use of a personal car on approved business trips is RM 0.60 per kilometre.
4.3 Parking and tolls are reimbursed at actual cost with receipts.
4.4 Commuting between home and the regular workplace is not reimbursable.

## 5. Items not reimbursable
5.1 Fines, penalties and traffic summonses.
5.2 Personal items, personal entertainment and expenses of family members.
5.3 Cryptocurrency or any payment made in digital assets.
