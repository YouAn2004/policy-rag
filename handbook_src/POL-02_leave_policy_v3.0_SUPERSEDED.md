# POL-02 Leave Policy (version 3.0, SUPERSEDED on 2026-01-01)

## 1. Annual leave
1.1 Employees with less than 5 years of service receive 14 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
1.2 Employees with 5 or more years of service receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
1.4 Up to 8 unused days may be carried forward to the next year. Carried forward days expire on 30 June.

## 3. Sick leave
3.1 Employees receive 14 days of paid sick leave per year when not hospitalised.
3.2 Employees receive up to 60 days of paid sick leave per year when hospitalised.

## 4. Parental leave
4.2 Paternity leave is 5 working days.
