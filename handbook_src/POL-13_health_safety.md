# POL-13 Health, Safety and Wellbeing Policy

## 1. Incident reporting
1.1 Any workplace accident, injury or near miss must be reported to Facilities at safety@meridianworks.example within 24 hours.
1.2 Serious injuries must be reported immediately by phone to extension 2999.

## 2. Emergency preparedness
2.1 Fire drills are held twice a year and attendance is mandatory.
2.2 There is at least one trained first aider for every 50 employees on each floor.
2.3 Employees must know their nearest exit and assembly point, shown on floor plans.

## 3. Ergonomics and wellbeing
3.1 Employees may request a workstation ergonomic assessment through the HR portal.
3.2 The Employee Assistance Programme gives free confidential counselling, up to 6 sessions per issue per year.
3.3 Smoking and vaping are not allowed inside company premises.
