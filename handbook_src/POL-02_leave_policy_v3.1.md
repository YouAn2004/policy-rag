# POL-02 Leave Policy (version 3.1)

## 1. Annual leave
1.1 Employees with less than 5 years of service receive 16 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
1.2 Employees with 5 or more years of service receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
1.3 Annual leave for new joiners is prorated by completed months in the first year.
1.4 Up to 5 unused days may be carried forward to the next year. Carried forward days expire on 31 March.
1.5 Unused leave is not paid out except on termination of employment, where accrued untaken leave is paid at the basic daily rate.

## 2. Applying for leave
2.1 Leave of 3 working days or fewer needs 3 working days notice.
2.2 Leave of more than 5 consecutive working days needs 14 calendar days notice and manager approval.
2.3 Managers should respond to leave requests within 3 working days.
2.4 Leave is requested through the HR portal.

## 3. Sick leave
3.1 Employees receive 18 days of paid sick leave per year when not hospitalised.
3.2 Employees receive up to 60 days of paid sick leave per year when hospitalised, inclusive of the 18 days.
3.3 A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required for sick leave of 2 or more consecutive days.
3.4 Employees must inform their manager before 10:00 on the first day of sick leave.

## 4. Parental leave
4.1 Maternity leave is 98 consecutive days.
4.2 Paternity leave is 7 working days and must be taken within 60 days of the birth.
4.3 Adoption leave follows the same entitlement as maternity or paternity leave, as applicable.

## 5. Other leave
5.1 Compassionate leave is 3 working days for the death of an immediate family member.
5.2 Marriage leave is 3 working days, once during employment.
5.3 Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be approved by the department head and HR for exceptional personal reasons.
5.4 This policy does not provide for sabbatical leave.

## 6. Contact
Leave questions go to hr@meridianworks.example or the HR helpdesk on extension 2100.
