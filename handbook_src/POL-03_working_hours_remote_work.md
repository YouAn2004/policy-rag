# POL-03 Working Hours and Remote Work Policy

## 1. Working hours
1.1 Standard working hours are 8 hours per day, Monday to Friday, with a 1 hour unpaid lunch break.
1.2 Employees may start between 07:00 and 10:00. Core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 must be worked by everyone.
1.3 Overtime must be approved in advance by the department head. Approved overtime is compensated as time off in lieu, not as cash.

## 2. Hybrid arrangement
2.1 The default arrangement is hybrid with at least 3 days per week in the office.
2.2 Up to 2 days per week may be worked from home with manager awareness through the team calendar.
2.3 Teams may set anchor days when everyone must be in the office. Anchor days are decided by the department head.

## 3. Working from abroad
3.1 Working from outside Malaysia is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year.
3.2 Working from abroad requires written approval from both the manager and HR at least 30 days in advance.
3.3 Employees must not work from abroad for any period without approval, because of tax, immigration and data protection implications.
3.4 The company may refuse a request where the destination creates legal or security risk.

## 4. Home office
4.1 Employees working from home must use a secure network and follow POL-06.
4.2 A one time home office allowance of RM 500 is available after confirmation of employment, claimable under POL-04.
4.3 Ergonomic assessments are available on request through POL-13.
