# POL-10 Compensation and Bonus Policy (RESTRICTED to HR, Finance and Executive)

## 1. Salary review
1.1 Salary reviews take place once a year in April.
1.2 Salary increases are linked to performance rating and position in the salary band.

## 2. Annual bonus
2.1 Target bonus is 5 percent of annual basic salary for Grades 1 to 3, 10 percent for Grades 4 to 6 and 15 percent for Grades 7 and 8.
2.2 The actual payout ranges from 0 to 150 percent of target, based on company and individual performance.
2.3 Bonuses are paid in March of the following year.
2.4 An employee must be employed and not serving notice on the payment date, and must have joined on or before 30 September of the performance year, to be eligible.
2.5 Employees on a PIP under POL-09 are not eligible.

## 3. Other payments
3.1 Employee referral bonus is RM 3,000, paid after the referred hire completes 6 months of service.
3.2 Long service award is RM 1,000 at 5 years and RM 2,500 at 10 years of service.
3.3 Salary bands for each grade are held by HR and must not be disclosed to employees outside HR, Finance and Executive.
