# POL-05 Business Travel Policy

## 1. Approval and booking
1.1 All business travel must be approved by the manager before booking.
1.2 Bookings must be made through the company travel desk at travel@meridianworks.example.
1.3 Overseas travel needs department head approval in addition to the manager.

## 2. Flights and accommodation
2.1 Economy class applies to all flights under 6 hours.
2.2 Premium economy or business class is allowed on flights of 6 hours or more only for employees at Director level and above.
2.3 Hotel nightly caps are RM 450 for Tier 1 cities and RM 300 for Tier 2 cities. Tier lists are held by the travel desk.
2.4 Exceeding a cap needs prior written approval from the department head.

## 3. Meals and allowances
3.1 Overseas meal allowance is RM 180 per day in Tier 1 cities and RM 130 per day in Tier 2 cities.
3.2 Domestic meal allowance during business travel is RM 70 per day.
3.3 Per diem allowances are paid without receipts. Other travel costs need receipts under POL-04.

## 4. Insurance and safety
4.1 Employees on overseas trips are covered by the company travel insurance. Policy details are held by HR.
4.2 Employees must register overseas trips with the travel desk so they can be contacted in an emergency.
4.3 Travel to destinations listed as high risk by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs needs CEO approval.

## 5. Contact
Travel desk at travel@meridianworks.example.
